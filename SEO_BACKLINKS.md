## 2. Google Business Profiles (the biggest lever for "salon near me")

Keep one profile per salon.

- **Website link:** that salon's page, for example `https://www.xingsalons.com/salons/hsr-layout?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`. The page's canonical tag removes the query, so it can't create a duplicate.
- **Categories:** Hair salon (primary), Beauty salon, Nail salon.
- **Services:** list them from the site's service menu, using the same names.
- **Photos:** post new ones every week, of the real salon and real work.
- **Posts:** one for every offer, with its real end date.
- **Reviews:** ask every client at checkout, with a QR code or a WhatsApp link. Reply to every review within a few days, including the bad ones. Never offer anything in exchange for a review; it breaks Google's rules.

## 4. Relationship links (earned, not bought)

- **Sister sites:** `xingtattoo.in` and `xingfitness.in` link to the salon in the footer and about page, with natural wording such as "Xing Premium Unisex Salon in Bangalore". The salon site already links back.
- **Brand salon locators:** only for brands the salon really uses. Olaplex is on the service menu; owner to confirm any others, such as L'Oréal Professionnel, and whether each locator is open to the salon.
- **Local media and community:** LBB, Curly Tales, Whitefield and HSR community pages, and RWA newsletters. Three angles:
  1. Why Bangalore's hard water is hard on hair, and what helps.
  2. How to choose a hair colour for Indian skin tones.
  3. One flat global colour price at any length, for the length of a real campaign.
- **Bridal directories:** WedMeGood and ShaadiSaga, for bridal hair and pre-wedding care.
- **Neighbourhood partners:** gyms, cafés and co-working spaces near each salon, with a shared offer or a link on each other's pages.
- **Guest expert posts:** a stylist writes for a local site, on a topic already covered on the blog.

## 6. Avoid

Each of these risks a Google penalty:

- Paid link packages
- Private blog networks (PBNs)
- Link exchanges at scale
- Fake reviews
- Keyword-stuffed anchor text
