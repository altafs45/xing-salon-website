We put a lot of care into our faces, but the skin on our arms, legs and back often gets forgotten. Sun, pollution and dryness leave it looking dull and feeling rough. A body polish is the body's equivalent of a good facial, and the **body polishing benefits** are simple: softer, smoother, glowing skin from head to toe.

This guide explains what body polishing is, what happens during a session, the occasions it's perfect for, and how to look after your skin afterwards.

## What Is Body Polishing?

Body polishing is a body treatment that gently exfoliates dull, dry skin with a scrub, then polishes and moisturises it. The result is skin that looks and feels smoother, softer and visibly glowing.

At Xing Premium Unisex Salon, our [body polish](/services/body-polish) is available for women, at all our salons in Bangalore.

## The Key Body Polishing Benefits

### 1. Removes dull, dry surface skin

The scrub gently lifts away the dull, dry surface skin that builds up on arms, legs and the back, especially after sun exposure.

### 2. Softer, smoother-feeling skin

Once the dull surface layer is gone, skin feels noticeably softer and smoother to touch.

### 3. A natural, healthy-looking glow

Polished, moisturised skin reflects light better, so it looks fresher and more radiant.

### 4. A relaxing self-care ritual

A full body polish is genuinely relaxing, especially with the complimentary head massage. Many clients book it simply as time for themselves.

### 5. Skin that's ready for events and outfits

Sleeveless blouses, backless outfits, dresses and holiday wear all show more skin. A body polish leaves that skin looking its best.

Here's what to expect step by step:

3. **Polish:** the skin is polished for a smooth finish.

Body polishing is popular for special occasions, but it works just as well as regular self-care.

## Before Your Body Polish

- **Avoid waxing or shaving** right before your appointment
- **Wear loose, comfortable clothes**
- **Tell your therapist** if you have sensitive skin, cuts, sunburn or a skin condition, or if you're pregnant

## Body Polish Aftercare

To keep your skin soft and glowing:

- **Moisturise daily** to keep skin soft
- **Use sunscreen** on exposed areas
- **Avoid waxing or shaving** right after your polish
- **Drink water** and keep skin hydrated
- **Avoid very hot showers** that dry the skin

## Body Polishing vs Other Treatments

It helps to know what body polishing is, and what it isn't.

- **Body polish vs de-tan:** a de-tan treatment helps reduce the look of tan from sun exposure. A body polish focuses on exfoliating, smoothing and moisturising for softer, glowing skin. They're separate services. Read [what a de-tan treatment is](/blog/what-is-detan-treatment) to learn more.
- **Body polish vs waxing:** waxing removes hair; body polishing smooths and refreshes skin. Many people do both before an event, with the timing advice above.
- **Body polish vs a facial:** a facial cares for the face. A body polish gives the rest of your skin similar attention.

## How Often Should You Get a Body Polish?

There's no fixed rule. Many clients book:

- **Before events and holidays**, one to three days before
- **Monthly**, as a self-care treat and to keep skin soft
- **Seasonally**, for example after summer, when skin feels dry from sun exposure

Your therapist can suggest a routine that suits your skin.

## A Note on Sensitive Skin

If you have sensitive skin, cuts, sunburn or a skin condition, or you're pregnant, let your therapist know before your session. They'll advise you on whether body polishing is suitable and when to book.

## Questions to Ask When You Book

- Which areas would you recommend for my occasion?
- How far ahead of my event should I book?
- Should I schedule waxing before or after, and how far apart?
- Is body polishing suitable for my skin?

## Common Mistakes

- **Waxing or shaving right before or after your polish**
- **Booking on the same day as a big event,** rather than one to three days before
- **Skipping moisturiser afterwards,** which lets skin dry out again
- **Forgetting sunscreen** on polished, exposed skin

## Body Polish for Different Skin Types

**Dry skin:** you'll notice the biggest difference, as the polish lifts dry, dull skin and the lotion restores softness. Moisturise daily afterwards.

**Oily or combination skin:** a polish helps body skin, especially the back, feel smoother and fresher.

**Sensitive skin:** tell your therapist first, so they can advise you and adjust the treatment.

## Making Your Glow Last

Moisturise every day, drink plenty of water and protect polished skin from strong sun. A gentle exfoliation once a week between salon visits also keeps skin smooth.
