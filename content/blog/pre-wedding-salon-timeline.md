A good schedule for your **pre-bridal salon services** means each treatment happens at the right time, with nothing rushed in the final week. A wedding is one of the few days when every detail is photographed, so your hair, skin and nails all deserve a little planning, and the earlier you start, the easier it is.

This guide gives you an eight-week timeline covering hair treatments, colour, spas, facials, waxing, body polish, nails and wedding-day hair styling, so you can plan your appointments with confidence.

## Why a Timeline Beats a Last-Minute Rush

Many treatments need time either to build results or to settle:

- **Hair treatments and colour** look most natural once they've had a few weeks to settle.
- **Facials**, especially a hydra facial series, build results over several sessions.
- **Waxing and threading** can leave mild redness that needs a few days to calm down.
- **Body polish and nail art** look freshest when they're done just before the day.

Spreading treatments out also means fewer long salon days in an already busy wedding month.

## Your Pre-Bridal Salon Services Timeline

### 6–8 weeks before: hair treatments and colour

Start with the treatments that change your hair the most.

- **Hair colour, highlights or balayage:** if you want a new shade or brighter dimension, do it now so the colour settles before the wedding. Consider adding Olaplex to protect your hair. Explore our [hair colour services](/services/global-hair-color).
- **Keratin, smoothening or other smoothing treatments:** if frizz is a worry, book a treatment now, not in the final weeks. Read [keratin vs smoothening vs straightening](/blog/keratin-vs-smoothening-vs-straightening) to choose.
- **Haircut:** decide on any change in length or shape early. Leave the final trim for closer to the day.

### 3–4 weeks before: glow and hair care

- **Hydra facial series:** start a course of the [7-Step Hydra Facial](/services/hydra-facial). For best results, book at least three sessions, three to four weeks apart.
- **Hair spa:** a [hair spa](/services/hair-spa) keeps hair soft and glossy, especially after colour or treatments. For best results, we recommend at least three sittings, two to three weeks apart.
- **Trial hairstyle:** share your outfit, jewellery and reference photos with your stylist and talk through the wedding-day look.

### About 1 week before: grooming

- **Waxing:** book about a week before, so any redness settles. Our [waxing services](/services/waxing) include flavored, roll-on cartridge and stripless options.
- **Threading:** neat brows and smooth facial skin.
- **De-tan:** helps skin look brighter and more even after sun exposure.
- **Final trim:** a light trim keeps ends fresh.

### A few days before: final facial

Book your last facial a few days before the wedding, not on the same day, so your skin has time to settle and glow.

### 1–3 days before: body polish and nails

- **Body polish:** a full [body polish](/services/body-polish), with shower, scrub, polish and nourishing lotion, leaves skin soft and glowing. Book it one to three days before. Our body polish is for women, and the full body polish includes a complimentary head massage.
- **Manicure and pedicure:** neat, soft hands and feet.
- **Nail art:** wedding nail art looks best done one to two days before. Popular bridal choices include French tips, chrome and stone-embellished designs. Explore our [nail art](/services/nail-art) styles.

Avoid waxing right before or right after a body polish.

### Wedding day: hair styling

Finish with bridal hair styling. Choose from:

- **Wash and blow dry:** smooth, bouncy, round-brush volume
- **Ironing:** sleek, straight, glossy hair
- **Tongs:** soft waves or defined curls

Explore our [bridal and party hair styling](/services/womens-haircut-styling), and read [blow dry vs ironing vs curls](/blog/blow-dry-vs-ironing-vs-curls) to choose your look.

## What to Avoid Close to the Wedding

- **Big colour changes in the final weeks.** Give colour time to settle.
- **First-time treatments.** Try anything new well before the wedding.
- **Waxing the day before.** Allow about a week.
- **Same-day facials.** Book a few days before.
- **New skin or hair products.** Stick to what you know works.

## Pre-Wedding Hair Care at Home

Salon treatments do the heavy lifting, but daily habits in the weeks before the wedding decide how your hair looks on the day.

- **Use a gentle, sulphate-free shampoo,** especially if your hair is coloured or treated.
- **Condition every wash and use a mask once a week** to keep lengths soft.
- **Protect your hair from heat.** Use a heat protectant before every blow dry, and avoid very hot tools in the final week.
- **Cover your hair in strong sun,** as sun dries hair and fades colour.
- **Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase** to reduce friction and frizz.
- **Avoid trying new hair products** close to the day.

If you're travelling for functions, carry a small kit: a wide-tooth comb, serum, pins and a heat protectant.

## Planning Several Functions?

Many Indian weddings have several events over a few days: haldi, sangeet, the wedding and the reception. A few tips:

- **Plan hair looks for each function together** at your style discussion, so they work with each outfit.
- **Schedule body polish before the first function** that needs it, and keep skin moisturised after.
- **Choose long-wearing nail options,** such as gel polish, if your functions are spread over several days.
- **Leave your hair as healthy as possible** between styled events: gentle washing and minimal heat.

## For Grooms, Family and Guests

Pre-wedding care isn't only for the bride. Grooms, family members and guests can follow the same timeline with haircuts, grooming, hair spas, threading and hair styling. Book group appointments early in wedding season.

## What to Bring to Your Appointments

A little preparation helps each appointment go smoothly:

- **For hair colour and styling:** photos of your outfit, jewellery and hairstyle ideas, plus any photos of looks you don't want.
- **For facials:** a list of products you use and any reactions you've had before.
- **For nail art:** your inspiration photos and outfit colours.
- **For body polish:** loose, comfortable clothes.
- **For every appointment:** your wedding and function dates, so we can space everything correctly.

Keeping all of this in one note on your phone saves time at every visit.

## Planning Tips

- **Book early.** Wedding season is busy, so reserve your key dates in advance.
- **Bring references.** Photos of your outfit, jewellery and hairstyle ideas help your stylist plan.
- **Share your schedule.** Tell us your wedding date and functions so we can space your appointments.
- **Keep a buffer.** Leave a day or two between treatments where you can.
