A beard can make you look sharper, older, more relaxed or more polished, depending on how it's kept. But walking into a salon, you'll often be asked whether you want a trim, a shave or styling. Knowing the difference helps you get exactly what your beard needs. Whether it's a quick **beard trim**, a clean shave or a fully sculpted style, here's how to choose.

## Beard Trim

**What it is:** clean lines on the cheeks and neck, an even length, and a shape that balances your face.

**Best for:** keeping your current beard neat.

A beard trim is regular maintenance. It doesn't change your beard style; it keeps it tidy, even and in proportion.

**Choose a trim if:**

- You're happy with your beard style but it's looking untidy
- Your neckline or cheek lines have grown out
- The length has become uneven

## Beard Shave

**What it is:** a smooth, close shave for a fresh, clean-shaven look.

**Best for:** a clean-shaven look.

**Choose a shave if:**

- You want to go clean-shaven for work, an event or a change
- You want a professional finish without razor bumps from rushing at home

## Beard Styling

**What it is:** defined sculpting for a specific beard style, from a short boxed beard to a fuller shape.

**Best for:** a defined, shaped beard style.

**Choose styling if:**

- You want a new beard shape
- You're growing your beard and want it shaped as it grows
- You want sharper, more defined lines than a regular trim

## Beard Shapes for Your Face

A beard can balance your face shape the way a good haircut does.

### Round face

**Aim for:** length at the chin and shorter sides, to make the face look longer.

**Try:** a beard with a defined, slightly longer chin, or a goatee-style shape with short sides.

### Long face

**Aim for:** width at the sides and a shorter chin.

**Try:** a fuller beard on the cheeks, kept shorter at the chin.

### Square face

**Aim for:** a beard that frames your strong jaw.

**Try:** an even, well-kept full beard or a short boxed beard, with slightly rounded edges to soften angles.

### Oval face

**Aim for:** almost anything.

**Try:** most styles work, from stubble to a full beard.

### Triangle face (wider jaw)

**Aim for:** keeping the sides neat and short.

**Try:** a shorter, tidy beard that doesn't add bulk at the jaw.

## Popular Beard Styles

- **Stubble:** short, even and low-maintenance; needs regular tidying to look intentional
- **Short boxed beard:** neat, defined and office-friendly
- **Full beard:** fuller and longer, shaped to suit your face
- **Goatee styles:** hair on the chin, with shaved or short cheeks

Bring a reference photo, and your stylist will adapt the style to your beard growth and face shape.

## How Often Should You Book?

It depends on your beard's growth and how sharp you like it:

- **Stubble and short beards:** need tidying often to stay neat
- **Short boxed and styled beards:** many men book a trim alongside their haircut, every 3–5 weeks
- **Full beards:** regular trims keep the shape and stop it looking untidy as it grows

Between appointments, you can tidy the neckline and cheek lines at home, but leave reshaping to your stylist.

## Keeping Your Beard Sharp at Home

- **Wash your beard** with a gentle cleanser
- **Use beard oil daily** to soften the hair
- **Comb it daily** to train the hair and spread oil
- **Tidy the neckline** between appointments
- **Use a small amount of product** if you style it

Our [beard care routine](/blog/beard-care-routine) explains each step.

## Common Mistakes

- **Trimming the neckline too high,** which makes the jaw look weaker
- **Over-trimming at home**, especially when reshaping
- **Ignoring the skin underneath,** which can get dry and itchy
- **Going too long between trims**, so the shape is lost

## Growing Out a Beard

If you're growing a beard for the first time:

- **Give it time.** Many beards fill in unevenly at first.
- **Resist trimming too early.** Let it grow for a few weeks before shaping.
- **Get a professional shape** once it has some length, so it looks intentional as it grows.
- **Use beard oil** to reduce itch in the early weeks.
- **Keep the neckline tidy** to look neat while you wait.

## Shaving Tips for a Clean-Shaven Look

If you prefer to be clean-shaven, a professional shave gives the smoothest finish. Between shaves at home:

- Shave after a warm shower, when hair is softer
- Use a sharp blade and a shaving cream or gel
- Shave with the direction of hair growth to reduce irritation
- Rinse with cool water and moisturise afterwards

If you get razor bumps often, mention it to your stylist.

## Your Beard and Your Haircut Together

Your beard and haircut should work as one look. A sharp fade often pairs well with a short, neat beard, while longer, textured hair suits a fuller beard. Booking both together means your stylist can balance the two and blend the sideburns properly.

## Beard Trim Etiquette at Home

If you tidy your beard between salon visits, set the neckline about one or two fingers above your Adam's apple, trim a little at a time, and always check both sides in the mirror. Leave the cheek lines and overall shape to your stylist, who can see your face from every angle.

## Beard Trims Before Events

Book a beard trim or shave a day or two before a wedding, interview or big event. That gives any redness time to settle, while your beard still looks freshly groomed on the day.
