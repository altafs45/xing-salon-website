Well-shaped eyebrows frame your face, and a small change in their shape can make a big difference to how you look. That's why **eyebrow threading** is one of the most popular quick salon services in India. It's precise, fast and works well on most skin types.

This guide explains how threading works, how to choose a brow shape that suits your face, how often to book, how threading compares with waxing and how to look after your skin afterwards.

## What Is Eyebrow Threading?

Threading uses a twisted cotton thread to lift hair from the root. The therapist rolls the twisted thread over the skin, catching hairs and removing them in neat lines. Because it works line by line, it gives very precise control over the shape.

**Why people choose threading:**

- **Precision:** ideal for defining brow shape, hair by hair
- **Speed:** a brow shape takes very little time
- **Clean lines:** for natural or sharp, defined brows
- **Works on small areas:** brows, forehead, upper lip, lower lip, chin and side locks

## Eyebrow Threading vs Waxing

Both remove hair from the root, but they suit different jobs.

| | Threading | Stripless Waxing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Method** | A twisted cotton thread lifts hair from the root | Stripless wax applied and removed |
| **Best for** | Precise brow shaping, small areas | Larger facial areas, very fine hair |
| **Precision** | Very high, line by line | Good for broader areas |

For brows, threading is usually the first choice because of its precision. For larger areas like the full face, stripless waxing is a popular alternative. Read our guide to the [types of waxing](/blog/types-of-waxing) to compare.

## Choosing a Brow Shape for Your Face

The best brow shape follows your natural brow and balances your face. Your therapist will look at your natural brow, but these general guidelines help.

### Round face

A soft, slightly higher arch helps add definition and lift. Very rounded brows can make the face look rounder.

### Oval face

Lucky you: most shapes suit an oval face. A soft, natural arch is a classic choice.

### Square face

A softer, rounded or gently curved arch balances a strong jawline.

### Heart-shaped face

A soft, rounded arch balances a wider forehead. Avoid very high, sharp arches.

### Long face

Flatter, straighter brows help the face look a little shorter and more balanced.

### A few universal tips

- **Follow your natural shape.** The most flattering brows usually enhance what you already have.
- **Don't over-thin.** Fuller brows look youthful and are easier to maintain. Over-threaded brows can take time to grow back.
- **Keep both brows balanced,** but remember that natural brows are rarely perfect twins.
- **Bring a reference photo** if you have a look in mind.

## Natural vs Defined Brows

Your therapist can shape your brows from natural and soft to sharp and defined:

- **Natural:** removes stray hairs while keeping your brow's shape and fullness. Great for everyday and for growing brows out.
- **Defined:** a clearer arch and cleaner lines. Polished and striking, especially for events and photos.

Tell your therapist which you prefer before they start.

## How Often Should You Get Your Eyebrows Threaded?

Most people book eyebrow threading **every 2–4 weeks**. It depends on:

- **How fast your hair grows**
- **How defined you like your brows**
- **Whether you're growing them out**

If you like very sharp, defined brows, you'll probably book more often. If you prefer a natural look, every few weeks may be enough.

### Growing your brows out

If you've over-plucked or want fuller brows, tell your therapist. They can tidy only the stray hairs outside your desired shape, while letting the rest grow.

## What to Expect at Your Appointment

1. **Discuss your brow shape** and the look you want
2. **Cleanse** the area
3. **Precise threading,** line by line
4. **Soothing toner** to calm the skin

You may feel a quick pinching sensation, but it's fast. Our therapists work precisely to keep it as comfortable as possible.

## Eyebrow Threading Aftercare

Freshly threaded skin can be a little red or sensitive. To keep it calm:

- **Avoid makeup, heavy creams and sun** on the area for a few hours
- **Don't touch or rub** freshly threaded skin
- **Keep the area clean**
- **Book your next brow threading in 2–4 weeks** to keep the shape neat

Redness usually settles quickly. If you have sensitive skin, active breakouts or a skin condition, tell your therapist before you start.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Threading too often.** Give hair time to grow so your therapist can shape properly.
- **Over-thinning.** Thin brows can be slow to grow back.
- **Threading right before an event** if your skin reddens easily. Leave a day or two.
- **Applying makeup straight away.** Give your skin a few hours.

## Eyebrow Threading for Men

Men's brows benefit from threading too, usually with a lighter touch. The aim is a clean, natural look: tidying stray hairs between and under the brows without changing their shape much. Tell your therapist you'd like a natural finish.

## Questions to Ask Your Therapist

- What shape suits my face and my natural brows?
- How much should I let grow before my next appointment?
- Can you keep my brows fuller?
- Is threading or waxing better for my upper lip or forehead?

## Threading Before Events

If your skin reddens easily, book eyebrow threading a day or two before an event rather than on the day. Redness usually settles quickly, but a little extra time means your skin looks calm in photos. For weddings, schedule threading about a week before, along with waxing and de-tan, and book a quick tidy-up a few days before if needed.

## Keeping Brows Neat Between Visits

Brush your brows upwards with a clean spoolie each morning, and resist the urge to pluck stray hairs at home, which can throw off the shape your therapist created.
