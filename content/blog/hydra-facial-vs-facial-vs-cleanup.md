"Should I get a facial or just a clean-up?" It's one of the most common questions at our front desk. Add hydra facials to the mix and it gets more confusing. The good news is that the **facial vs clean up** decision is simple once you know what each treatment does and what your skin needs right now.

This guide compares clean-ups, classic facials and hydra facials side by side, with a simple way to choose and a sensible routine for keeping your skin in good shape.

## The Quick Answer

- **Clean-up:** your skin feels dull or congested and you want a quick freshen-up.
- **Classic facial:** you want pampering, nourishment and glow.
- **Hydra facial:** you want the biggest glow and hydration, or you have an event coming up.

## What Is a Clean-Up?

A clean-up is a quicker treatment focused on cleansing and freshening your skin. It removes grime and refreshes tired-looking skin, which makes it ideal between facials.

At Xing Premium Unisex Salon, we offer two clean-ups:

- **Masque Clean-up:** a quick cleanse-and-mask that removes grime and leaves skin fresh and clear.
- **Premium Clean-up:** a more thorough clean-up for congested or tired-looking skin, leaving it cleaner, smoother and refreshed.

**Choose a clean-up if:** your skin feels dull or congested, you want regular upkeep, or you're short on time.

## What Is a Classic Facial?

A facial goes further than a clean-up. Along with cleansing, it includes massage, nourishment and masks for a deeper glow and real relaxation.

Our classic facials come in three levels:

- **Express Facial:** a time-saving facial that cleanses, nourishes and brightens.
- **Premium Facial:** a complete facial with deeper nourishment and a relaxing massage.
- **Ultra Facial:** our most indulgent classic facial for intensive pampering and a refreshed, glowing complexion.

**Choose a facial if:** you want to relax, your skin needs nourishment, or you want a glow that lasts beyond a quick refresh.

## What Is a Hydra Facial?

A hydra facial combines deep cleansing, gentle exfoliation, extraction and intense hydration in one session. At Xing, our 7-Step Hydra Facial is our bestselling facial. Most people notice brighter, smoother, more hydrated-looking skin right after the first session.

**Choose a hydra facial if:** your skin is dull, dehydrated or congested, or you want visible glow before an event. Our guide to [hydra facial benefits](/blog/hydra-facial-benefits) explains it in detail.

## What Happens During a Facial at Xing

A classic facial broadly follows these steps:

1. **Skin check**
2. **Cleanse**
3. **Exfoliate and steam**
4. **Treatment, massage and mask**
5. **Moisturise and protect**

A clean-up is shorter and focuses on the cleansing and mask stages. A hydra facial moves through seven steps that focus on cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration.

## How Often Should You Book?

A sensible routine for most people:

- **Clean-up:** every 2–3 weeks
- **Facial:** every 3–4 weeks
- **Hydra facial:** at least three sessions, three to four weeks apart, then regular maintenance

Many people alternate: a facial or hydra facial once a month, with a clean-up in between. Your therapist can suggest a routine that suits your skin and schedule.

## Signs Your Skin Needs a Clean-Up, Facial or Hydra Facial

Sometimes it's easier to start from what you see in the mirror:

**You probably need a clean-up if:**

- Your skin looks grimy or dull after a busy week of commuting
- You notice more blackheads or a congested T-zone
- It's been two or three weeks since your last treatment

**You probably need a facial if:**

- Your skin looks tired and you feel tired too
- You want to relax as much as refresh
- Your skin feels like it needs nourishment, not just cleansing

**You probably need a hydra facial if:**

- Your skin looks dull and feels tight or dehydrated
- Your pores look congested and your texture feels uneven
- You have an event coming up and want visible glow

## Building a Simple Monthly Routine

Most people get the best results from a mix rather than one treatment repeated. A sample month:

| Week | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Facial or 7-Step Hydra Facial |
| Week 3 | Clean-up |
| Week 5 | Facial or hydra facial again |

In between, keep a simple home routine: a gentle cleanser, moisturiser and daily sunscreen. That combination keeps skin clean, hydrated and glowing month after month.

## Adjusting for the Season

Your skin's needs change through the year, so your choice of treatment can too.

- **Summer:** more sweat, oil and sun. Regular clean-ups keep congested skin fresh, and daily sunscreen matters most.
- **Monsoon:** humidity can leave skin feeling oily but still dehydrated. A hydra facial balances cleansing with hydration.
- **Winter:** cooler, drier air leaves skin tight. A nourishing classic facial, like the Premium or Ultra, adds comfort and glow.

Whatever the season, your therapist's skin check on the day is the best guide to what your skin needs.

## Booking for an Event

Planning for a wedding, party or photoshoot? Book your facial **a few days before** the event, not on the same day. This gives your skin time to settle and glow. For bigger events, start a hydra facial series a few weeks earlier. Our [pre-event skin prep timeline](/blog/pre-event-skin-prep-timeline) lays out a full plan.

## Aftercare for Any Facial or Clean-Up

- Avoid heavy makeup for the rest of the day
- Skip scrubs and strong actives for a few days
- Wear sunscreen daily
- Drink water and keep your skin moisturised

### Before you book

Tell your therapist if you have sensitive skin, active breakouts, a skin condition or recent skin treatments, or if you're pregnant. They'll adjust the treatment or advise you on timing. If you have an ongoing skin concern, consult a dermatologist.
