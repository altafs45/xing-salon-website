Bangalore's rains are a welcome break from the heat, but they're hard on hair. Humidity turns smooth hair frizzy within minutes, damp air leaves hair flat and dull, and a scalp that stays wet for hours can feel itchy. A few changes to your routine make the season much easier. Good **monsoon hair care** is mostly about managing moisture: keeping it in your hair, and out of your scalp.

This guide explains why hair behaves differently in the rains, and gives you a practical wash routine, scalp care tips, styling advice and the salon treatments that help.

## Why Hair Changes in the Monsoon

### Humidity causes frizz

When the air is humid, dry or damaged hair absorbs moisture from it and swells unevenly. The outer layer of the hair lifts, and hair puffs up and frizzes. Wavy and curly hair is naturally more prone to this.

### Damp hair loses shape

Blow dries and curls drop faster in humid air. Hair can look flat at the roots and frizzy at the ends at the same time.

### The scalp stays damp

Getting caught in the rain, tying up damp hair or sweating in humid weather leaves the scalp damp for long periods. Many people notice more itchiness and flaking in the monsoon.

### Rainwater and pollution

Rain can carry dust and pollution, leaving hair feeling grimy.

## Monsoon Hair Care: Your Wash Routine

### Wash regularly, gently

Many people need to wash a little more often during the rains, especially after getting caught in rainwater or after workouts. Use a gentle, sulphate-free shampoo so you clean the scalp without stripping the lengths.

### Condition every time

Conditioner smooths the outer layer of the hair, which helps reduce frizz. Apply it to the lengths and ends, not the scalp, and rinse well.

### Use a weekly mask

Dry hair frizzes most. A weekly hydrating mask keeps hair moisturised so it absorbs less moisture from the air.

### Rinse with cool or lukewarm water

Hot water lifts the hair's surface and makes frizz worse. A cooler final rinse helps.

### Dry your hair properly

Don't leave hair damp for hours, and don't tie it up wet. Gently squeeze out water with a soft towel and dry it fully, especially at the roots.

## Monsoon Scalp Care

A healthy scalp is especially important in the rains.

- **Wash after getting soaked.** Rainwater can leave the scalp grimy.
- **Dry your scalp properly** before tying your hair.
- **Go easy on heavy oils.** Oil left on a damp scalp for long periods can make some people's scalps feel itchier.
- **Don't scratch.** Massage gently instead.
- **Keep combs and towels clean and dry.**

If your scalp becomes flaky or itchy, our guide to [dandruff causes and treatment](/blog/dandruff-causes-treatment-bangalore) explains what helps. If the issue persists, consult a dermatologist.

## Styling Tips for Humid Days

### Use a light serum

A few drops of a light serum on damp ends helps smooth the surface and reduce frizz. Avoid heavy products that weigh hair down.

### Always use heat protectant

If you blow dry or iron, use a heat protectant. Keep tools on a moderate setting.

### Choose low-maintenance styles

On very humid days, styles that work with the weather are easier:

- **Braids**, which keep frizz contained
- **A low bun or twisted bun**
- **A sleek ponytail with a soft tie**
- **Half-up styles** that keep hair off your neck

### Carry a monsoon hair kit

- A small wide-tooth comb
- A light serum
- A few soft ties and pins
- An umbrella or a scarf

### Avoid tight ties on damp hair

Tight ties on damp hair can leave dents and cause breakage. Use soft, fabric ties.

## Monsoon Hair Care for Coloured Hair

Coloured and lightened hair is more porous, so it frizzes more and can fade faster in damp, polluted air. Use a colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo, a weekly mask and heat protection. A Nourishing or Olaplex Hair Spa keeps coloured hair healthier through the season.

## Monsoon Hair Care for Men

## A Quick Monsoon Hair Care Checklist

**Every wash**

- Gentle, sulphate-free shampoo
- Conditioner on the lengths
- Cool or lukewarm rinse
- Dry hair and scalp properly

**Every week**

- A hydrating mask
- Clean combs and towels

**Every day you style**

- Light serum on damp ends
- Heat protectant before heat styling
- Soft ties, never on wet hair

**Every few weeks**

- A Moroccan or Anti-Dandruff hair spa

## Common Monsoon Hair Mistakes

- **Tying up wet hair**, which can leave the scalp damp and cause breakage
- **Skipping conditioner** because hair feels limp
- **Using heavy products** that weigh hair down and attract grime
- **Leaving hair damp after getting caught in the rain**
- **Over-styling with heat** to fight frizz, which dries hair further

## Monsoon Hair Care for Curly and Wavy Hair

Curly and wavy hair reacts most to humidity. A few extra tips help:

- **Use more conditioner** and leave a little in, if your hair likes it
- **Apply a light styling cream or serum** to damp hair to define curls
- **Avoid brushing dry curls,** which creates frizz; detangle in the shower with conditioner instead
- **Scrunch, don't rub,** with a soft towel or cotton T-shirt

## Getting Caught in the Rain

It happens to everyone in Bangalore. When your hair gets soaked:

- Rinse with clean water as soon as you can, to remove rainwater and pollution
- Wash and condition if your scalp feels grimy
- Squeeze, don't rub, your hair dry
- Dry your scalp and roots properly before tying your hair
- Avoid leaving hair damp under a helmet or scarf for hours
