## What Is a De-tan Treatment?

A de-tan treatment is a skin treatment that helps reduce the look of tan caused by sun exposure. It works on the surface of the skin to leave it looking fresher, brighter and more even.

It's important to be clear about what a de-tan does and doesn't do:

- **It helps reduce the look of tan** from sun exposure
- **It leaves skin looking brighter and more even**
- **It doesn't change your natural skin tone**
- **Results vary** with your skin type and how much sun exposure you've had

## Sun Protection: Keeping Tan Away

A de-tan helps reduce the look of existing tan, but sun protection is what keeps it from coming back. These daily habits make the biggest difference.

### Wear sunscreen every day

- Apply sunscreen every morning, even on cloudy days
- Use enough to cover your face, neck, ears and any exposed skin
- Don't forget your hands, arms and feet if they're exposed
- Reapply when you're outdoors for a long time

### Cover up in strong sun

- Wear a hat or cap and sunglasses
- Choose long sleeves or a light scarf on long days outdoors
- Use a helmet visor and gloves if you ride a two-wheeler
- Choose covered footwear on long walks if sandal tan lines bother you

### Moisturise regularly

Sun-exposed skin can feel dry. Moisturising daily keeps it soft and comfortable.

### Avoid peak sun when you can

The sun is strongest in the middle of the day. Plan outdoor activities for the morning or evening when possible.

## Tan Prevention for Commuters

- Keep a small sunscreen in your bag or car for reapplying
- Use sleeves or gloves when riding
- Cleanse your face as soon as you get home to remove sweat, dust and sunscreen

## How Often Should You Get a De-tan?

There's no fixed schedule, because it depends on how much sun exposure you have and how quickly tan builds up again. Many people book:

- **After a holiday** or a long stretch outdoors
- **Before an event,** about a week ahead
- **Alongside regular facials or clean-ups,** for a fresh, even-looking finish

Daily sunscreen makes the biggest difference to how often you need one.

## Common Mistakes

- **Skipping sunscreen after a de-tan,** which lets tan build up again quickly
- **Scrubbing hard at home** to remove tan, which can irritate skin
- **Booking a de-tan the day before an event** if your skin reacts easily
- **Not mentioning sensitive skin or a skin condition** to your therapist

## De-tan for Feet and Hands

Feet are often forgotten, but sandal tan lines are one of the most common reasons people book a de-tan. For hands, apply sunscreen to the backs of your hands too, especially if you ride a two-wheeler.
