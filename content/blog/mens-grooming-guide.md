Men's grooming doesn't have to be complicated. A few simple daily habits and the right salon services at the right time keep you looking sharp without spending hours on it. Whether you're new to grooming or want to upgrade your routine, this guide covers the basics: what to do at home, and what a good **men's grooming salon** can do for your hair, beard and skin.

## The Daily Basics

Most of your grooming happens at home, and it only takes a few minutes.

### Morning

1. **Cleanse your face** with a gentle face wash, not body soap
2. **Moisturise** with a light, non-greasy moisturiser
3. **Apply sunscreen** to your face and neck, every day
4. **Style your hair** with a small amount of product
5. **Comb your beard** and apply a few drops of beard oil, if you have one

### Evening

1. **Cleanse your face** to remove sweat, dust and sunscreen from the day
2. **Moisturise**

That's it. Consistency matters far more than having lots of products.

- **Use a face wash,** not body soap, on your face
- **Moisturise,** even if your skin is oily

## Hair Care Basics for Men

- **Wash with a gentle shampoo** and rinse well
- **Use conditioner,** even on short hair, if it feels dry
- **Use a small amount of styling product**

## Grooming on a Budget of Time

Short on time? Prioritise these:

1. **Daily sunscreen.** The single most useful skin habit.
2. **A regular haircut.** A sharp cut does more for your look than any product.
3. **A tidy beard or a clean shave.** Half-grown, untidy stubble is the most common grooming mistake.
4. **Clean, trimmed nails.** A quick Cut & File keeps hands looking neat.

Everything else is a bonus.

## Grooming for Different Hair Types

**Thick hair:** benefits from a cut that removes bulk and a little product for control. A hair spa every few weeks keeps it soft.

**Fine or thinning hair:** shorter, textured cuts look fuller. Use a matte product and avoid heavy gels that clump hair.

**Curly or wavy hair:** can look great with the right cut. Use conditioner and avoid brushing curls when dry.

**Grey hair:** looks distinguished with a sharp cut. If you prefer to cover greys, ask about colour.

## Grooming for Different Skin Types

**Oily skin:** a gentle foaming face wash and a light, gel-based moisturiser.

**Dry skin:** a non-foaming cleanser and a richer moisturiser, especially in drier months.

**Sensitive skin:** fragrance-free products, introduced one at a time. Shave with care, and tell your stylist if your skin reacts to shaving.

## Grooming for Work and Interviews

A neat, conservative haircut, a well-kept beard or a clean shave, and clean, trimmed nails make a strong first impression. Book your haircut a few days before an interview, so it has time to settle and looks natural rather than freshly cut.

## Grooming Through the Seasons

**Summer:** wash your face and beard more often, keep hair shorter if you like, and never skip sunscreen. **Monsoon:** dry your hair and beard properly after getting caught in the rain, and keep your scalp clean to avoid itching. **Winter:** switch to a richer moisturiser and keep up the beard oil, as drier air leaves skin tight and flaky.
