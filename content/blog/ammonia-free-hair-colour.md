"Ammonia-free" is one of the most common phrases you'll see on hair colour, and many people assume it means natural, chemical-free or completely gentle. The truth is more nuanced. **Ammonia free hair colour** is a genuinely popular, gentler choice for many people, but it helps to understand what it actually means before you book.

This honest guide explains what ammonia does in hair colour, what changes when it's removed, the real pros and limits, and who tends to prefer it.

## What Does Ammonia Do in Hair Colour?

Many permanent hair colours use ammonia. In simple terms, it helps open up the outer layer of the hair so the colour can get inside. That's part of how traditional permanent colour achieves strong, lasting results.

Some people notice that ammonia-based colour has a strong smell, and some find it less comfortable on a sensitive scalp.

## What Is Ammonia Free Hair Colour?

Ammonia free hair colour is colour made without ammonia. It uses a different approach to help colour work, without the strong ammonia smell.

- **Ammonia-free root touch-up:** root regrowth coverage with an ammonia-free formula

## The Honest Pros of Ammonia-Free Colour

- **A gentler option:** many people choose it because they prefer a gentler formula, especially if they colour regularly
- **Less strong smell:** many clients find the experience more pleasant
- **Good for regular upkeep:** popular for frequent root touch-ups and grey coverage
- **Full, even colour:** it can give even, polished results

## The Honest Limits

Being clear about the limits helps you make the right choice:

- **It's not chemical-free.** It's still a chemical hair colour, just made without ammonia.
- **It's not allergy-free.** You can still react to other ingredients, so a patch test is wise if you're new to colour or have sensitive skin.
- **It may not suit every goal.** Some results, such as going much lighter, involve lightening that works differently. Your stylist will tell you what's achievable.
- **Results vary with your hair.** Your natural base, the number of greys and your colour history all affect the result.

That's why every colour service starts with a consultation.

## Who Prefers Ammonia-Free Colour?

Ammonia-free colour is a popular choice for:

- **People who colour often,** such as for regular grey coverage
- **People who prefer a gentler formula**
- **People who dislike the smell** of traditional colour
- **Men and women covering greys** with global colour or root touch-ups

## Ammonia-Free vs Regular Permanent Colour

| | Ammonia-Free Colour | Regular Permanent Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Formula** | Made without ammonia | Usually contains ammonia |
| **Smell** | Usually milder | Can be stronger |
| **Popular for** | Regular upkeep, grey coverage, a gentler option | Strong, lasting colour changes |
| **Patch test** | Still recommended if new to colour or sensitive | Recommended if new to colour or sensitive |

Your stylist will recommend the right formula for your hair, your greys and the result you want.

## Patch Tests: Don't Skip Them

If it's your first time colouring, you've had a reaction before, or you have sensitive skin, ask us about a patch test before your appointment. It's a simple step that helps you colour with confidence, whichever formula you choose.

## Protecting Your Hair, Whatever the Formula

Colouring, ammonia-free or not, can leave hair drier over time. To keep coloured hair healthy:

- **Use a colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo**
- **Wash with cool or lukewarm water**
- **Use a heat protectant** before styling
- **Protect your hair from strong sun**
- **Book a hair spa every few weeks**
- **Add Olaplex** if your hair is lightened or feels weak

Our guide on [how to make hair colour last longer](/blog/make-hair-colour-last) explains each habit.

## Common Myths About Ammonia-Free Colour

**"Ammonia-free means natural."** It's still a chemical hair colour, just without ammonia.

**"Ammonia-free can't cover greys."** At Xing, both global colour and root touch-ups are available in ammonia-free versions for grey coverage.

**"I don't need a patch test with ammonia-free."** You still should if you're new to colour or have sensitive skin.

**"It works the same for every shade."** Results depend on your base, your greys and the shade you want. Your stylist will advise.

## How to Decide: Ammonia-Free or Regular?

A few practical questions help you choose:

**How often do you colour?** If you colour every few weeks for grey coverage, many people prefer the gentler, ammonia-free option for regular upkeep.

**What result do you want?** For a rich, even shade close to your natural colour, or for covering greys, ammonia-free colour is often a good fit. For a big change, such as going much lighter, your stylist may recommend a different approach.

**How does your scalp feel?** If traditional colour has felt uncomfortable on your scalp, mention it. Your stylist will consider ammonia-free options and recommend a patch test.

**How many greys do you have?** The number and spread of greys can affect how coverage looks. Your stylist will check before recommending a formula and shade.

Ammonia-free colour is cared for exactly like any other colour. Gentle, colour-safe washing and regular touch-ups keep it looking fresh, and a hair spa every few weeks keeps coloured hair soft.
