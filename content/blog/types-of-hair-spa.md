Walk into any salon and you'll see a list of hair spas with names that don't always explain much. Nourishing, Moroccan, Tribond, Olaplex: which one is right for you? Understanding the **types of hair spa** makes the choice simple, because each one is designed for a specific concern.

This guide walks through the main types, who each one suits and how to pick the right one, so you can walk into your appointment knowing what to ask for.

## First, Identify Your Main Concern

Before looking at spa names, ask yourself what bothers you most about your hair right now. Most people fall into one or two of these groups:

- **Dry and dull:** hair feels rough and lacks shine
- **Frizzy:** hair puffs up, especially in humid weather
- **Weak and brittle:** hair snaps easily or feels thin and fragile
- **Scalp issues:** flaking, itching or a scalp that feels unclean
- **Breakage:** lots of short broken hairs when you comb
- **Chemical damage:** hair that's been coloured, bleached or heat-styled a lot

Once you know your main concern, the right spa is usually obvious.

### Nourishing Hair Spa

**Best for:** dry, dull, rough hair.

The Nourishing Hair Spa is a deep-conditioning ritual that replenishes moisture. If your hair feels like straw at the ends or looks flat and lifeless, this is usually the best place to start. It leaves hair soft and glossy and is a good all-rounder for most hair types.

### Moroccan Hair Spa

**Best for:** frizzy hair, especially in humid weather.

The Moroccan Hair Spa is an indulgent spa that helps tame frizz and adds silky shine and smoothness. It's popular in Bangalore during the monsoon, when humidity makes frizz hard to control. If your hair is dry *and* frizzy, your stylist may suggest Moroccan over Nourishing.

### Tribond Hair Spa

**Best for:** weak, brittle or over-styled hair.

The Tribond Hair Spa is a strengthening spa for hair that needs extra care. If your hair snaps easily, feels thin or has been through a lot of heat styling, Tribond focuses on making it feel stronger and more resilient.

### Anti-Dandruff Hair Spa

**Best for:** a flaky, itchy scalp.

The Anti-Dandruff Hair Spa is a clarifying scalp treatment. It helps reduce flakes and itchiness and leaves your scalp feeling clean and balanced. It focuses on the scalp rather than the lengths. If the issue persists, consult a dermatologist, since ongoing dandruff can have causes a spa alone won't address.

### Anti-Hairfall Hair Spa

**Best for:** hair fall caused by breakage.

The Anti-Hairfall Hair Spa is a scalp-focused spa that nourishes the roots and helps reduce breakage-related hair fall. It's important to understand the difference between breakage (hair snapping along its length) and hair loss from the root. A spa can help with breakage, but hair loss can have many causes. If you notice thinning or hair fall that continues, consult a dermatologist.

### Olaplex Hair Spa

**Best for:** coloured, highlighted or bleached hair.

The Olaplex Hair Spa uses Olaplex bond-building care to help repair and strengthen hair damaged by colouring, bleaching or heat. If you've had balayage, highlights or a big colour change, this is the spa that targets that kind of damage. Read more in our guide on [what Olaplex is and who needs it](/blog/what-is-olaplex).

## Add-Ons Worth Knowing About

### Olaplex Add-on

You don't have to choose between a spa for your concern and bond repair. You can add Olaplex to any hair spa for extra repair and protection. For example, if your coloured hair is also frizzy, a Moroccan spa with an Olaplex add-on covers both.

### Head Massage

A calming scalp massage on its own, for when you mainly want to relieve stress and feel refreshed.

## What If You Have More Than One Concern?

Many people do. Coloured hair is often dry. Frizzy hair is often weak at the ends. Hard water can cause both a dry scalp and rough lengths.

In these cases, your stylist will usually:

1. Deal with the most important concern first
2. Add Olaplex if your hair is chemically treated
3. Plan your next spa to target the second concern, if needed

## Which Type of Hair Spa for Which Hair?

A few common situations, and the spa we'd usually suggest:

- **"My hair is long and the ends feel like straw."** Nourishing, possibly with an Olaplex add-on.
- **"My hair puffs up every time it rains."** Moroccan.
- **"I iron my hair every morning and it keeps breaking."** Tribond, plus a heat protectant at home.
- **"I have white flakes on my shoulders."** Anti-Dandruff.
- **"I see lots of short broken hairs in my comb."** Anti-Hairfall.
- **"I've just had balayage and my hair feels rough."** Olaplex Hair Spa.

## Looking After Your Hair Between Spas

Whichever spa you choose, the results last longer when your home routine supports them:

- **Dry or coloured hair:** a sulphate-free shampoo and a weekly mask.
- **Frizzy hair:** a light serum on damp ends and a heat protectant before styling.
- **Weak, brittle hair:** a wide-tooth comb, less heat and regular trims.
- **Flaky scalp:** rinse thoroughly, avoid very hot water and don't scratch.
- **Hard water at home:** a shower filter helps. See our guide to [hard water hair damage](/blog/hard-water-hair-damage-bangalore).
