You have a wedding, a party or an important day at work coming up, and you want your hair to look its best. Salon styling is the easiest way to get a polished finish, but which style should you choose? The **blow dry vs ironing** question, with curls as the third option, comes down to the finish you want, the occasion and how your hair behaves.

This guide explains each style, the occasions it suits best, how to help it hold, and how to prepare your hair before your appointment.

- **Wash & Blow Dry:** a smooth, bouncy, round-brush blow dry with salon volume.
- **Moroccan/Defabulous Blow Dry:** a nourishing blow dry that adds shine and softness while taming frizz.

All are available for short, shoulder-length, below-shoulder and waist-length hair. Here, we'll focus on the three main finishes: blow dry, ironing and curls.

## The Blow Dry

**The finish:** smooth, bouncy, full of volume. A round brush lifts the roots and shapes the ends, so hair looks healthy, glossy and full of movement.

**Best for:**

- Office events and meetings
- Dinners and casual parties
- Daytime functions
- Any time you want polished but natural-looking hair

**Who it suits:** almost everyone. It works especially well if you want more volume or have fine hair that falls flat.

**Frizz-prone hair?** Ask about the Moroccan/Defabulous Blow Dry, which adds shine and softness while taming frizz.

## Ironing

**The finish:** sleek, straight and glossy. A flat iron smooths each section, so hair looks polished and reflects a lot of light.

**Best for:**

- Formal events and evening functions
- Sleek, modern outfits
- Photoshoots where you want a clean, polished look
- Showing off a fresh colour, as straight hair shows shine best

**Who it suits:** most hair types, especially if you love a sleek look. Very fine hair may look flatter when ironed; your stylist can keep some movement at the ends if you prefer.

## Curls and Waves (Tongs)

**The finish:** soft waves or defined curls, depending on the look you want. Curls add volume, movement and a romantic, special-occasion feel.

**Best for:**

- Weddings and receptions
- Sangeet, engagement and festive functions
- Parties and photoshoots
- Any time you want hair that looks dressed up

**Who it suits:** most lengths. Longer hair shows waves and curls beautifully; shorter hair can have soft, tousled waves.

| | Blow Dry | Ironing | Curls & Waves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Finish** | Smooth, bouncy, voluminous | Sleek, straight, glossy | Soft waves or defined curls |

## How to Choose for Your Occasion

### Think about your outfit

- **Heavy embroidery or a traditional outfit:** soft curls or waves often balance it beautifully.
- **A sleek dress or modern saree drape:** ironed hair looks clean and elegant.
- **Smart casual or office wear:** a bouncy blow dry is the safe, polished choice.

### Think about the weather

Bangalore's monsoon humidity can loosen curls and make straight styles frizz. On humid days:

- Ask your stylist about frizz control, such as the Moroccan/Defabulous Blow Dry
- Consider a half-up style that keeps hair off your neck
- Carry a small comb and a few pins

### Think about the length of your event

For long events, ask your stylist how to help your style hold. Some looks can be pinned or adjusted as the day goes on.

## Styling by Hair Length

All three styles work on every length, but each length has its strengths.

### Short hair

A blow dry gives short cuts shape and lift at the roots. Ironing makes bobs look sharp and sleek. Soft, tousled waves add texture and movement.

### Shoulder-length hair

The most versatile length. A bouncy blow dry with flicked ends, a sleek ironed finish or loose waves all work well.

### Below-shoulder hair

Waves and curls really show at this length, and a round-brush blow dry gives beautiful volume and movement.

### Waist-length hair

Long hair carries waves and curls beautifully and looks dramatic ironed straight. It takes more time to style, so mention your length when you book.

## Quick Touch-Ups During Your Event

Carry a small kit for long events:

- **A wide-tooth comb** for curls; a fine comb for sleek styles
- **A few pins** to secure loose sections
- **A little serum** for flyaways
- **A small clip** to pin hair up if it gets warm

Avoid brushing curls through, as it will loosen them; use your fingers instead.

## How Long Will Your Style Hold?

There's no fixed answer, and we won't promise one. How long a style holds depends on:

- **Your hair type:** very fine or very straight hair can lose curls faster; thick hair can hold curls well.
- **The weather:** humidity, rain and heat loosen any style.
- **Your activities:** dancing, sweating and travelling all take a toll.
- **How you treat it:** touching and brushing your hair a lot loosens the style.

Your stylist can suggest ways to help your style last through your event.

## How to Prepare Your Hair

A little preparation helps any style look and hold better:

- **Come with clean, dry hair,** or book a wash as part of your styling.
- **Skip heavy oil the day before,** as it can weigh hair down and stop curls holding.
- **Bring reference photos** of the look you want.
- **Tell your stylist about your outfit and the event,** so they can suggest the right finish.
- **Book in advance** for weddings and festive season.

## Protecting Your Hair from Heat

All three styles use heat. Occasional styling is fine, but frequent heat can dry hair out over time. At the salon, your stylist uses heat protection. At home:

- Always use a heat protectant before blow drying, ironing or curling
- Keep tools on a moderate setting
- Give your hair heat-free days
- Book a hair spa to keep hair nourished

- **Haldi or mehendi-day functions:** a simple blow dry or loose waves
- **Sangeet:** bouncy curls or waves for dancing
- **Wedding:** soft, romantic waves or curls
- **Reception:** sleek ironed hair or glamorous curls

If your ends look tired, a quick trim before styling makes any finish look sharper. Many clients book a haircut and styling together before a big event.
