If your hair is dry, frizzy or damaged, you've probably come across two popular treatments: hair botox and keratin. Both promise softer, smoother, shinier hair, so it's easy to assume they're the same. They aren't. Understanding **hair botox vs keratin** comes down to one question: is your main problem damage, or frizz?

This guide explains what each treatment does, the finish you can expect, who each one suits and why hair botox has nothing to do with injections.

## The Quick Answer

- **Choose hair botox** if your hair is dry, damaged, dull or coloured and you want it to look fuller, glossier and softer, without changing its texture much.
- **Choose keratin** if frizz is your main concern and you want smoother, more manageable hair that keeps its natural volume.

Now let's look at each one properly.

## What Is Hair Botox?

Hair botox is an intensive conditioning treatment. It helps fill and smooth damaged, dry hair for a fuller, glossier, softer finish. Think of it as a deep repair treatment rather than a smoothing or straightening one.

### Hair botox involves no injections

This is the most common misunderstanding, so it's worth being clear. Hair botox:

- Is applied to your hair, not injected anywhere
- Uses no injections and no botulinum toxin
- Is a hair-care treatment, not a cosmetic injection

The name simply describes its smoothing, filling effect on damaged hair, in the same way people use "botox" loosely to mean smoothing.

### Hair botox benefits

- Helps repair the feel of dry, damaged hair
- Adds shine and softness
- Makes hair look fuller and healthier
- Causes minimal change to your natural texture

## What Is a Keratin Treatment?

A keratin treatment smooths the surface of the hair to control frizz and add a glossy shine. It keeps your hair's natural body and movement, so it doesn't leave hair flat. It's designed for people who like their volume but want to stop fighting frizz.

### Keratin benefits

- Controls frizz, even in humid weather
- Adds glossy shine
- Makes daily styling faster
- Keeps natural volume and movement

For more on how keratin compares with smoothening and straightening, read [keratin vs smoothening vs straightening](/blog/keratin-vs-smoothening-vs-straightening).

## Hair Botox vs Keratin: Side by Side

| | Hair Botox | Keratin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main goal** | Repair and shine | Frizz control and smoothness |
| **Finish** | Fuller, glossy, soft | Smooth, with natural volume |
| **Best for** | Dry, damaged or coloured hair | Frizzy hair, keeping volume |
| **Texture change** | Minimal | Smoother, less frizz |
| **How it wears** | Depends on hair type, growth and aftercare | Gradually softens over time |

### The simplest way to decide

Look at your hair on a normal day and ask what bothers you most:

1. **"It feels dry, rough and damaged."** Hair botox.
2. **"It frizzes and puffs up, especially in humidity."** Keratin.
3. **"Both."** Talk to your stylist. They'll prioritise the bigger problem and may suggest a hair spa alongside.

## Who Should Choose Hair Botox?

Hair botox is a good fit if:

- Your hair has been coloured, highlighted or bleached
- Your ends feel dry and straw-like
- Heat styling has left your hair rough and dull
- You want healthier-looking hair, not straighter hair
- You want to keep your natural texture

## Who Should Choose Keratin?

Keratin is a good fit if:

- Frizz is your number one complaint
- Your hair takes ages to blow dry and still frizzes
- You want smoother hair with natural volume
- You don't want a poker-straight look

## What About Cysteine and Nanoplastia?

These are two more treatments that often come up in the same conversation:

- **Cysteine** is a smoothing treatment that reduces frizz and makes hair easier to manage, while keeping a soft, natural look. It suits people with frizzy hair who want to stay natural.
- **Nanoplastia** is a smoothing and straightening treatment for frizzy, wavy or curly hair. It gives a silky, straighter, natural-looking finish.

| **Main goal** | Repair and shine | Frizz control and smoothness | Smoother, straighter hair |

## Hair Botox vs Keratin by Hair Type

Your hair type often points to the right choice.

### Fine or thin hair

Fine hair can look flat easily. Keratin keeps natural volume while controlling frizz, and hair botox can make fine hair look fuller and glossier. Your stylist will consider how much smoothing your hair actually needs.

### Thick, frizzy hair

Thick hair that puffs up in humidity usually responds well to keratin. If it's also very dry or damaged from colour, your stylist may suggest a hair spa alongside.

### Coloured or bleached hair

Colour and bleach weaken hair from the inside. Hair botox focuses on repair and shine, which suits chemically treated hair. Adding Olaplex to a spa is another option for bond repair.

### Wavy or curly hair

If you want to keep your waves but lose the frizz, keratin or cysteine may suit you. If you'd like your hair noticeably straighter, ask about smoothening, straightening or nanoplastia instead.

## Questions to Ask Your Stylist

- What's my hair's main problem: damage, frizz or both?
- How much will this change my hair's texture?
- How will the result wear over time?
- What aftercare will I need?
- Does my colour history change which treatment is safe?

## Planning Treatments Around Hair Colour

If you colour your hair, tell your stylist before booking either treatment. The order and spacing of colour and smoothing treatments matter, and your colour history can change which treatment is the safest choice. Your stylist will plan both services so your hair stays healthy and your colour looks its best.

## Common Myths

**"Hair botox is an injection."** It isn't. It's a conditioning treatment for your hair.

**"Keratin makes hair flat."** Keratin is designed to keep natural volume. If you want flat, poker-straight hair, straightening or nanoplastia is closer to what you're after.

**"One treatment fixes everything."** Each treatment has a focus. Matching the treatment to your main concern is what gets the best result.
