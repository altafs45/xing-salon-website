If you've ever coloured, highlighted or bleached your hair, someone has probably recommended Olaplex. It's one of the most talked-about names in hair care, but many people aren't sure what it actually does. Put simply, an **Olaplex treatment** helps repair hair that has been weakened from the inside by colour, bleach or heat.

This guide explains what bond repair means in plain language, who benefits from Olaplex, how it fits with colour services, and the difference between an Olaplex spa and an Olaplex add-on.

## Why Hair Gets Weak in the First Place

To understand Olaplex, it helps to know a little about how hair is built.

Each strand of hair is held together by tiny connections called bonds. They give hair its strength and stretch. When bonds are healthy, hair is strong, flexible and shiny.

Some things break these bonds:

- **Bleaching and lightening**, which are needed for highlights, balayage and lighter shades
- **Colouring**, especially repeated colour over the same lengths
- **Heat styling**, like daily ironing, curling and hot blow drying
- **Some chemical treatments** that change the hair's texture

When enough bonds are broken, hair becomes weak. It feels rough and stretchy when wet, snaps easily, tangles more and loses its shine. Regular conditioner can make it feel smoother on the surface, but it doesn't reach the bonds inside.

## What Is an Olaplex Treatment?

An Olaplex treatment is bond-building care. Instead of only coating the outside of the hair, it works to help repair and strengthen the hair from within.

In simple terms:

- **Conditioners and masks** make hair feel softer on the surface.
- **Olaplex** focuses on the inner structure, helping restore strength to damaged hair.

That's why Olaplex is often recommended alongside colour and lightening, which are the services most likely to weaken those inner bonds.

## Who Benefits Most from Olaplex?

An Olaplex treatment is most useful if your hair is:

- **Coloured**, especially if you colour regularly
- **Highlighted, balayaged or bleached**
- **Heat-styled often**, such as daily ironing or curling
- **Breaking easily**, feeling stretchy when wet or rough when dry
- **Going through a big colour change**, such as going lighter or trying fashion shades

If your hair is healthy and simply a little dry, you may not need Olaplex. A nourishing hair spa may be all your hair needs. Your stylist will help you decide at your consultation.

## How Often Should You Get an Olaplex Treatment?

This depends on how damaged your hair is and how often you colour or heat-style it. A few general guidelines:

- **With every colour or lightening service:** an Olaplex top-up helps protect your hair at the point it's most at risk.
- **When repairing damaged hair:** a short course of spas works better than a single one. For hair spas, we recommend at least three sittings, two to three weeks apart, then monthly maintenance.
- **For maintenance:** add Olaplex to your regular spa if your hair is coloured or bleached.

Your stylist will suggest a plan that suits your hair.

## What Olaplex Can and Can't Do

It's worth having realistic expectations.

**Olaplex can help:**

- Make damaged hair feel stronger and less fragile
- Reduce breakage from colour, bleach and heat
- Improve how hair feels and behaves after chemical services

**Olaplex can't:**

- Undo all damage in one session if hair is badly broken
- Replace trims, as split ends still need cutting
- Make up for harsh daily habits, like very hot tools without heat protection

The best results come from Olaplex combined with good aftercare.

## Signs Your Hair Could Use Bond Repair

Not sure if your hair is damaged enough to need Olaplex? These are common signs:

- **It stretches when wet** and doesn't spring back
- **It feels rough or gummy** after lightening
- **It snaps easily** when brushed or styled
- **It tangles more than it used to**
- **It looks dull** even after conditioning
- **The ends feel straw-like** and split quickly

If several of these sound familiar, and especially if your hair is coloured or bleached, an Olaplex Hair Spa or an Olaplex add-on is worth discussing with your stylist. They can check your hair and tell you how much repair it needs.

## Aftercare That Makes Olaplex Work Harder

To get the most from your Olaplex treatment:

- Use a colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo
- Always use a heat protectant before blow drying, ironing or curling
- Lower the heat on your tools where you can
- Rinse with cool or lukewarm water
- Book regular hair spas to keep lengths nourished
- Get regular trims to remove split ends
