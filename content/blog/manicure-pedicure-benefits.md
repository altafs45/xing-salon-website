Hands and feet do a lot of work every day, and they rarely get much attention in return. Dust, sun, closed shoes, typing, cooking and cleaning all leave them dry, rough and tired. A manicure or pedicure is one of the simplest ways to look after them, and the **manicure pedicure benefits** go beyond pretty nails.

This guide explains what you gain from regular manicures and pedicures, what happens in a session, how to choose a ritual and how often to book.

## The Key Manicure Pedicure Benefits

### 1. Neat, healthy-looking nails

Regular shaping keeps nails even and tidy, and stops snags that lead to breaks. Clean, shaped nails look polished even without colour.

### 2. Cared-for cuticles

Cuticles protect the base of your nails. Gentle cuticle care keeps them neat, so nails look cleaner and hangnails are less likely.

### 3. Softer hands

Exfoliation and moisturising leave hands noticeably softer, especially if you wash your hands often or work with your hands.

### 4. Smoother feet

Pedicures exfoliate rough skin on the feet and heels, leaving them smoother and more comfortable. For very rough, dry heels, ask about our Heel Peel Treatment. Read [how pedicures and heel treatments help rough heels](/blog/rough-heels-pedicure-care).

### 5. Real relaxation

The soak and massage are genuinely relaxing. After a long week, a pedicure is one of the easiest ways to unwind.

### 6. A polished look for work and events

Neat hands and feet finish any look, whether it's an important meeting, a wedding or a holiday.

## How Often Should You Book?

### Pedicures

Every **3–4 weeks** works well for most people and helps keep heels and cuticles in good shape. In summer, or if you wear open footwear often, you may want to book at the shorter end.

### Manicures

How often depends on your routine. Many people book every few weeks to keep cuticles neat and nails tidy. If you wear gel polish, book around your removal and refresh.

### Before events

Book your manicure and pedicure one to three days before a wedding, party or holiday, so your nails look fresh on the day. If you're adding nail art, book it one to two days before.

## Keeping Hands and Feet Looking Great Between Visits

- Moisturise hands and feet daily, especially heels
- Wear gloves for cleaning and washing dishes
- Use cuticle oil a few times a week
- Let polish dry fully before slipping on closed shoes
- Book a pedicure every 3–4 weeks to keep heels smooth

## Who Should Check First?

If you have diabetes, circulation issues, cuts or a nail or skin infection, tell your therapist before your session. They'll take extra care or advise you on timing. For ongoing foot or nail concerns, consult a doctor.

## Manicure Pedicure Benefits Through the Year

- **Summer:** open footwear shows feet more, and sweat and dust dry out skin. Regular pedicures keep feet fresh.
- **Monsoon:** damp feet and closed shoes can leave feet tired. A pedicure helps them feel clean and refreshed.
- **Winter:** drier air leaves hands and heels rough. Nourishing rituals like Crystal or Alga help.

## Common Questions

**Can I wear closed shoes after a pedicure?** Yes, once your polish is fully dry. Bring open footwear if you can.

**How long does polish last?** It depends on your polish type and how you use your hands. Gel polish is long-wearing; regular polish is easier to change.

**Are manicures only for women?** Hand and foot care benefits everyone. Ask your salon which services are available for you.
