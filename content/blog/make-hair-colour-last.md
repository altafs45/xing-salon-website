A fresh colour looks its best the day you leave the salon. Keeping it that way takes a little effort at home. Good **hair colour care** is mostly about avoiding the everyday habits that strip colour, and giving coloured hair the extra moisture and strength it needs.

This guide covers what to do after your appointment, which products to use, how water, heat and sun affect your colour, and how hair spas and Olaplex help your colour last longer.

## Why Hair Colour Fades

Colour molecules sit inside the hair. Every time the outer layer of the hair lifts, some of that colour can escape. Several things make that happen:

- **Harsh shampoos** with strong cleansers
- **Hot water**, which lifts the hair's outer layer
- **Heat styling**, especially at high temperatures
- **Strong sun**
- **Chlorine** from swimming pools
- **Hard water**, which can leave minerals that dull colour and push it towards brassy tones

Damaged, porous hair also loses colour faster than healthy hair. That's why caring for the health of your hair is part of caring for your colour.

## Hair Colour Care: The First Few Days

### Wait before your first wash

Wait at least a couple of days before washing your hair after colouring. This gives the colour time to settle.

### Skip heavy heat styling

Avoid hot tools for the first few days if you can. If you need to style, use a heat protectant and the lowest heat that works.

### Keep hair out of pools

Avoid swimming for the first days after colouring, as chlorine can strip fresh colour quickly.

## Products That Protect Your Colour

### Colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo

This is the most important change. Sulphate-free shampoos clean more gently and strip less colour. Look for ones labelled for coloured hair.

### A good conditioner, every wash

Conditioner smooths the hair's outer layer, which helps keep colour in. Apply it to the lengths and ends.

### A weekly mask

Coloured hair needs extra moisture. A rich mask once a week keeps it soft and shiny.

### Heat protectant

Use one every time you blow dry, iron or curl.

### Leave-in or serum

A few drops on the ends add shine and protect against dryness.

## Washing Habits That Help

- **Wash with cool or lukewarm water.** Hot water lifts the outer layer and lets colour escape.
- **Wash a little less often** if your scalp allows. Fewer washes mean less fading.
- **Shampoo the scalp, not the lengths.** Let the lather run through the lengths as you rinse.
- **Finish with a cool rinse** to help smooth the hair.

### If you have hard water

Hard water is common in many Bangalore homes, and minerals can dull colour and make it turn brassy. A shower filter helps. Our guide to [hard water hair damage](/blog/hard-water-hair-damage-bangalore) explains more. Ask your stylist before using a clarifying shampoo, as some can fade colour.

## Protecting Colour from Sun and Water

Bangalore's sun is stronger than it feels, and colour fades faster with regular sun exposure.

- **Cover up.** A hat or scarf protects your colour on long days outdoors.
- **Before swimming,** wet your hair with clean water, wear a cap if you can, and rinse and condition straight afterwards.
- **After a beach holiday,** book a hair spa to restore moisture.

## How Hair Spas and Olaplex Help Colour Last

### Hair spas

Colouring can leave hair dry and porous. A regular [hair spa](/services/hair-spa) restores moisture and smoothness, which helps colour look richer and shinier. For coloured hair, a Nourishing or Olaplex Hair Spa often works well.

### Olaplex

Colouring and lightening can weaken the bonds inside the hair. Olaplex is a bond-building treatment that helps repair and strengthen it. At Xing, you can add an Olaplex top-up to any [hair colour service](/services/global-hair-color), or add Olaplex to any hair spa. Read more in [what is Olaplex](/blog/what-is-olaplex).

## Colour-Specific Tips

### Global colour and grey coverage

Regrowth is the main thing that makes global colour look less fresh. Most people book a root touch-up every 4–6 weeks, depending on how fast their hair grows and how visible their greys are. Our guide on [how often you need a root touch-up](/blog/root-touch-up-frequency) explains more.

### Balayage and highlights

Lightened hair is more fragile and can turn brassy. Colour-safe products, heat protection and regular spas keep it bright and healthy. For more on these looks, read [balayage vs highlights](/blog/balayage-vs-highlights).

### Fashion and pastel shades

Vivid and pastel shades fade fastest of all. Wash with cool water, wash less often and expect to refresh them more regularly. Ask your stylist about the best way to maintain your shade.

### Ammonia-free colour

If you've chosen ammonia-free colour, the same care applies. Gentle shampoo, cool water and heat protection help it last.

## Common Hair Colour Care Mistakes

Even with good products, a few habits undo your efforts:

- **Washing too soon after colouring.** Give colour at least a couple of days to settle.
- **Using any shampoo you have at home.** Many everyday shampoos contain strong cleansers that strip colour.
- **Very hot showers.** They feel good but fade colour quickly.
- **Skipping heat protectant.** High heat fades colour and dries out lightened hair.
- **Ignoring brassiness.** Warm, orange tones on lightened hair are a sign it's time for a gloss or refresh, not something to live with.
- **Swimming without preparation.** Chlorine can strip colour within a few swims.

## Seasonal Hair Colour Care in Bangalore

**Summer:** strong sun is the biggest risk. Cover your hair outdoors and keep up your masks.

**Monsoon:** humidity makes coloured hair frizz more. A Moroccan or Nourishing hair spa helps keep it smooth.

**Winter:** cooler, drier air can leave lengths dry. Add a weekly mask and a little more leave-in conditioner.

## When to Book a Colour Refresh

Signs it's time to visit:

- Your roots or greys are clearly visible
- Your colour looks dull or brassy
- Your ends feel dry and the colour looks uneven
- Your balayage or highlights have lost their brightness

Book a little before these become obvious, and your colour will always look fresh.

## A Simple Hair Colour Care Routine

1. **After colouring:** wait at least a couple of days before your first wash.
2. **Every wash:** colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo, conditioner, cool or lukewarm water.
3. **Every week:** a hydrating mask.
4. **Every time you style:** heat protectant.
5. **Every few weeks:** a hair spa, with Olaplex if your hair is lightened.
6. **Every 4–6 weeks (for greys or global colour):** a root touch-up.

## Hair Colour Care for Men

Men's colour, whether it's grey coverage, highlights or beard colour, fades for the same reasons. Shorter hair is cut more often, so colour is refreshed more naturally, but gentle shampoo, cooler water and sun protection still help. Regular root touch-ups keep grey coverage looking natural rather than patchy.
