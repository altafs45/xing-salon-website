Blue, pink, purple, red, pastel lilac: bold hair colour is a fun way to express yourself. But vivid shades behave very differently from natural colour, especially on dark Indian hair. Before you book **fashion hair colour**, it helps to understand pre-lightening, how quickly these shades fade and how to keep your hair healthy.

This guide covers everything to know before going bold, from low-commitment options to aftercare.

## What Is Fashion Hair Colour?

Fashion colour, sometimes called crazy colour, means vivid or unconventional shades that aren't found in natural hair. Popular choices include:

- **Vivid shades:** blue, pink, purple, red, green
- **Pastels:** soft lilac, baby pink, mint, peach
- **Deep jewel tones:** wine, plum, emerald

At Xing Premium Unisex Salon, our fashion colour is applied per foil, so you can choose anything from a single peek-a-boo strand to a bold statement look.

## Pre-Lightening: The Most Important Thing to Know

Most Indian hair has a dark natural base. Vivid and pastel shades show best on light hair, so they usually need **pre-lightening** first.

### What pre-lightening means

Pre-lightening lifts your natural colour to a lighter shade, so the fashion colour on top shows true. Without it, many shades simply won't show on dark hair, or will look muddy.

### Why it matters

- **Pastels need the lightest base.** Soft shades like lilac and baby pink need hair lifted quite light.
- **Vivid shades need less.** Deep reds, purples and blues can sometimes show on a less lightened base.
- **Lightening can weaken hair.** That's why bond care like Olaplex matters.

Your stylist will confirm during your consultation whether pre-lightening is needed for your shade, based on your natural base and colour history.

## Low-Commitment Ways to Go Bold

Not ready for a full head of colour? Try these first:

- **Peek-a-boo strand:** a hidden section underneath that shows when you move
- **A few streaks:** bold pieces through your hair
- **Face-framing pieces:** colour around the face for maximum impact
- **Tips only:** colour on the ends, which is easy to cut off later

These options let you try fashion colour with less lightening, less upkeep and an easy way back.

## Fashion Colour Fades Faster

This is the reality of bold colour: vivid and pastel shades fade faster than natural shades. Every wash, hot shower and day in the sun takes a little colour away.

### What affects fading

- **How often you wash**
- **Water temperature:** hot water fades colour quickly
- **Your shampoo:** strong cleansers strip colour
- **Sun exposure**
- **Chlorine:** swimming can strip vivid colour quickly
- **Hard water**, which can dull colour

### Expect to refresh

Plan for more frequent refreshes than with natural colour. Your stylist can advise how to maintain your shade.

## Choosing a Shade That Suits You

Skin undertone still matters with bold colour:

- **Warm undertones** often suit coppery reds, warm pinks, corals and peachy pastels
- **Cool undertones** often suit blues, purples, cool pinks and silvery pastels
- **Neutral undertones** can wear most shades

## Protecting Your Hair During Lightening

### Add Olaplex

Olaplex is bond-building care that helps protect, repair and strengthen hair during and after colouring and lightening. At Xing, you can add an Olaplex top-up to any [hair colour service](/services/fashion-color). Read [what is Olaplex](/blog/what-is-olaplex) for more.

### Book regular hair spas

Lightened hair can feel dry and fragile. A regular [hair spa](/services/hair-spa), such as a Nourishing or Olaplex Hair Spa, keeps it soft and healthy.

### Be honest about your colour history

Tell your stylist about any previous colour, henna, home dye or treatments. They affect how your hair lightens and which shades are achievable.

## Fashion Colour Aftercare

To keep bold colour bright for longer:

- **Wait at least a couple of days** before your first wash
- **Wash with cool water,** and less often if your scalp allows
- **Use a colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo**
- **Use a heat protectant** and keep hot tools on a moderate setting
- **Protect your hair from sun and chlorine**
- **Use a weekly mask** to keep lightened hair soft

Our guide on [how to make hair colour last longer](/blog/make-hair-colour-last) has more tips.

## Thinking About Going Back to Natural?

When you're ready to move on from bold colour, talk to your stylist. Depending on your shade and how much it has faded, they can suggest a new colour, a gradual transition or a cut to remove coloured ends. Low-commitment options like tips and streaks are the easiest to grow out.

## Questions to Ask Before You Book

- Will I need pre-lightening for this shade?
- How light does my hair need to go?
- How quickly will this shade fade, and how often will I refresh it?
- Should I start with streaks or a peek-a-boo strand?
- Should I add Olaplex?
- What should I use at home?

## Planning Fashion Colour Around Your Life

Bold colour is fun, but it helps to think practically before you book:

- **Work and events:** if your workplace or upcoming events need a more classic look, start with a peek-a-boo strand that you can hide or show.
- **Swimming:** if you swim often, vivid colour will fade faster. Plan for refreshes, or choose deeper jewel tones that tend to hold up better than pastels.
- **Upkeep budget of time:** pastels in particular need more frequent refreshes. Choose a shade you're happy to maintain.
- **Your natural hair health:** if your hair is already very dry or damaged, your stylist may suggest a few hair spas first, so your hair is in better shape for lightening.

## Fashion Colour for Men

Bold colour isn't just for women. Men often start with tips, a few streaks or a single colour on the top section, especially on shorter cuts where colour shows clearly. Shorter hair is cut more often, so it's easy to change or remove colour if you want to try something new. Our hair colour services are available for men and women.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- **Choosing a pastel on very damaged hair** without preparing it first
- **Washing with hot water** and fading your colour in days
- **Skipping Olaplex** during lightening
- **Expecting vivid colour to last like natural colour**
- **Not mentioning henna or home dye,** which can affect how hair lightens

## Fashion Colour Before an Event

Planning bold colour for a party, festival or photoshoot? Book it a few days ahead rather than the day before, so your stylist has time to adjust the shade if needed and your colour has settled. Remember that the first few washes fade vivid shades fastest.

Fashion hair colour is a fun way to express yourself, as long as you plan for pre-lightening, faster fading and a little extra care. Start small with streaks or a peek-a-boo strand, protect your hair with Olaplex and regular spas, and enjoy your colour.
