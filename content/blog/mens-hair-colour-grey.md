Greys appear at different ages for different men, and how you handle them is entirely your choice. Some men embrace the silver; others prefer to cover it. If you'd like to colour, the goal for most men is simple: look like yourself, just with fewer greys. Good **men's hair colour** is about choosing the right technique and shade so the result looks natural, not "dyed".

This guide covers the options, from global colour and highlights to ammonia-free colour and beard colour, plus how to keep your colour looking fresh.

- **Global Color (Ammonia-Free):** the same all-over colour in an ammonia-free option

- **Beard Color:** evens out greys or patchy tones for a fuller-looking beard

## Global Colour

**What it is:** one even shade applied all over to cover greys or refresh your colour.

**Best for:**

- Covering greys across your whole head
- A uniform, polished look

**Grow-out:** regrowth shows at the roots as your hair grows. Shorter cuts can show it sooner, so regular touch-ups keep coverage natural.

## Highlights to Blend Greys

**What it is:** lighter strands that add contrast and dimension.

**Best for:**

- Blending greys rather than covering them completely
- A textured, natural, multi-tonal look
- Softer grow-out

If you have some greys and don't want a solid, all-over colour, highlights can make greys growing in look more natural.

## Global Colour vs Highlights

| | Global Colour | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Look** | One even shade | Dimension and contrast |
| **Greys** | Covered | Blended |
| **Grow-out** | Visible regrowth at the roots | Softer, more gradual |
| **Best for** | Full coverage, a uniform look | A natural, textured look |

Not sure? Your stylist can recommend one based on how many greys you have and the look you want.

## Ammonia-Free Colour for Men

If you prefer a gentler formula, ask for **ammonia-free global colour**. It's a popular choice for men who colour regularly. It isn't chemical-free or allergy-free, so if it's your first time colouring or you have sensitive skin, ask about a patch test before your appointment. Read our guide to [ammonia-free hair colour](/blog/ammonia-free-hair-colour) for more.

## Beard Colour

Greys often show in the beard first, sometimes before your hair. **Beard colour** evens out greys or patchy tones for a fuller-looking beard.

**Tips for a natural beard colour:**

- Match your beard to your hair colour, or go very slightly lighter
- Avoid very dark shades if your beard is patchy, as they can make gaps more obvious
- Keep up regular touch-ups as your beard grows

## Choosing a Natural-Looking Shade

The biggest mistake men make is going too dark. A natural result usually means:

- **A shade close to your natural colour,** or slightly lighter
- **Avoiding jet black,** unless that's your natural shade
- **Considering your skin tone:** warm or cool shades should suit your undertone

A shade close to your natural colour also grows out more softly, so regrowth is less obvious.

## How Often Will You Need a Touch-Up?

It depends on how fast your hair grows, how many greys you have and your haircut. Short cuts show regrowth faster, simply because there's less length to hide it. Many men book colour touch-ups alongside their haircut, every 3–5 weeks, so both stay fresh. Read [grey hair coverage options](/blog/grey-hair-coverage-options) for more on planning upkeep.

## Keeping Men's Hair Colour Fresh

- **Use a colour-safe shampoo** to help colour last longer
- **Wash with cool or lukewarm water**
- **Protect your hair from strong sun**
- **Use a small amount of styling product**
- **Book touch-ups** as your hair grows

Our guide on [how to make hair colour last longer](/blog/make-hair-colour-last) has more tips.

## Fashion Colour for Men

If you want something bolder, fashion colour lets you try a different shade. Many men start with tips, a few streaks or colour on the top section. Most fashion shades need pre-lightening on dark hair. Read [fashion hair colour](/blog/fashion-hair-colour-guide) to learn more.

## Common Men's Hair Colour Mistakes

- **Going too dark**, which looks unnatural
- **Mismatched hair and beard colour**
- **Using box colour at home**, which can be hard to match and apply evenly
- **Waiting too long between touch-ups**
- **Skipping colour-safe shampoo**

## Embracing the Grey

Colouring is a personal choice. If you prefer to keep your greys, a sharp haircut and a well-kept beard make grey hair look distinguished. Your stylist can suggest a cut that suits silver hair.

## Grey Coverage for Short Haircuts

Short cuts, fades and crops show regrowth quickly, because there's very little length to hide greys at the roots. A few tips:

- **Book colour with your haircut,** so both stay fresh together
- **Choose a shade close to your natural colour,** so regrowth is softer
- **Consider highlights** to blend greys if you want fewer visits
- **Keep the sides and back in mind:** greys often show first around the temples and sideburns

## Colour and Your Beard Together

Many men colour their hair but forget their beard, or the other way round. If your hair and beard don't match, the result can look less natural. Your stylist can:

- Match your beard to your hair colour
- Blend a greying beard rather than covering it completely
- Suggest whether to colour hair, beard or both, based on where your greys are

## Is Men's Hair Colour Right for You?

Ask yourself:

1. **How do I feel about my greys?** If they don't bother you, there's no need to colour.
2. **How many greys do I have?** A few may be best blended; many may suit global colour.
3. **How often can I visit?** Global colour needs regular touch-ups; highlights are lower maintenance.
4. **Do I want a subtle or noticeable change?** Start subtle if you're unsure.

## First Time Colouring?

If it's your first time, start with a shade close to your natural colour. You can always go further at your next appointment. Ask about a patch test if it's your first time colouring or you have sensitive skin.
