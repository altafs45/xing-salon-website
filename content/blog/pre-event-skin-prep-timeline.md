Good **pre-event skin care** starts weeks before the day. Glowing skin rarely comes from a single last-minute treatment; it comes from a little planning. A simple timeline means your skin is calm, hydrated and glowing when it matters, without any last-minute surprises.

Whether it's a wedding, an engagement, a festival, a party or a photoshoot, this guide gives you a week-by-week plan, explains which salon treatments to book and when, and lists what to avoid in the final days.

## Why Does Timing Matter?

Skin needs time. Some treatments build results over several sessions. Others can leave mild redness or sensitivity that needs a few days to settle. Booking everything the day before is the most common mistake, and the easiest one to avoid.

A good timeline:

- Gives treatments like a hydra facial series time to build results
- Leaves room for skin to settle after waxing, threading and facials
- Avoids trying anything new when there's no time to recover

## Your Pre-Event Skin Care Timeline

### 4–6 weeks before: start your facial series

This is the best time to begin. A [7-Step Hydra Facial](/services/hydra-facial) combines deep cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration, and most people notice brighter skin after the first session. Results build with regular treatments, so we recommend at least three sessions, three to four weeks apart.

Also use this time to:

- Settle into a simple daily routine: gentle cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen
- Drink plenty of water
- Book your salon appointments early, especially around wedding season

### 3–4 weeks before: keep up your sessions

Book your next hydra facial or [facial](/services/facial-cleanup). If you prefer a classic facial, a Premium or Ultra facial adds nourishment and relaxation. A clean-up in between keeps skin fresh. Not sure which to choose? Read [hydra facial vs facial vs clean-up](/blog/hydra-facial-vs-facial-vs-cleanup).

### 1–2 weeks before: tidy up

- **Waxing and threading:** book these about a week before, so any redness has time to settle.
- **De-tan:** if your skin has a tan from the sun, a de-tan treatment helps skin look brighter and more even.
- **Hair:** this is a good time for a hair spa, so your hair looks its best in photos too.

### A few days before: your final facial

Book your last facial or hydra facial a few days before the event, not on the same day. This gives your skin time to settle and glow.

### 1–3 days before: body polish and nails

- **Body polish:** for soft, smooth, glowing skin on your arms, back and legs, book a full body polish one to three days before. Our body polish is for women.
- **Manicure and pedicure:** neat hands and feet finish the look.
- **Nail art:** book one to two days before the event.

Avoid waxing right before or right after a body polish to keep your skin comfortable.

### The day of the event

Keep it simple:

- Cleanse gently and moisturise
- Wear sunscreen if you'll be outdoors
- Drink water
- Don't try anything new

## What Should You Avoid in the Final Week?

The last few days are not the time for experiments. Avoid:

- **New skin products.** A new serum or cream can cause a reaction you don't have time to fix.
- **Strong peels or harsh scrubs.** They can leave skin red or sensitive.
- **First-time treatments.** If you've never had a particular treatment, try it well before the event, not days before.
- **Same-day facials.** Give your skin a few days to settle.
- **Waxing the day before.** Allow about a week.
- **Late nights and dehydration.** They show on your skin quickly.

## Your Daily Routine in the Weeks Before

Salon treatments work best when your daily routine supports them. Keep it simple and consistent:

**Morning**

1. A gentle cleanser
2. A light moisturiser
3. Sunscreen, every day, even when it's cloudy

**Evening**

1. Cleanse well to remove sunscreen, sweat and pollution
2. Moisturise

**Every week**

- One gentle exfoliation, if your skin tolerates it (stop in the final week)
- A hydrating mask if your skin feels tight

Consistency matters more than adding more products. If your routine already works, don't change it close to the event. A routine you follow every day beats an elaborate one you only manage now and then.

## Lifestyle Habits That Show on Your Skin

- **Sleep:** late nights show up as dullness and puffiness quickly.
- **Water:** staying hydrated helps skin look fresher.
- **Sun:** a tan or sunburn in the final weeks is hard to undo, so cover up and wear sunscreen.
- **Stress:** easier said than done before a big event, but regular breaks, walks and a relaxing facial genuinely help.

## Short on Time?

Not everyone gets six weeks' notice. If your event is soon:

- **One week away:** book a hydra facial now, waxing and threading in the next day or two, and a body polish one to three days before.
- **A few days away:** a hydra facial or Express Facial, plus mani-pedi. Skip waxing if your skin is sensitive.
- **Tomorrow:** focus on hydration, sleep and a gentle routine. Avoid anything new.

## If Your Skin Is Sensitive

Sensitive skin needs a little more care before an event, but it can still glow.

- **Start earlier.** Give yourself extra time between treatments in case your skin needs to settle.
- **Do a test run.** If you haven't had a hydra facial or a particular wax before, try it well ahead of the event, not in the final week.
- **Choose gentler options.** Stripless wax is often preferred for sensitive and delicate areas like the face and underarms.
- **Keep products simple.** Fewer products mean fewer chances of a reaction.
- **Tell your therapist.** Mention any reactions you've had before, so they can adjust the treatment.

If you have a skin condition, speak to a dermatologist before starting a new routine.

## Skin Care for Grooms and Guests

Pre-event prep isn't only for brides. Grooms, family and guests can follow the same timeline with clean-ups, facials, threading and grooming. If you have a wedding coming up, our [pre-wedding salon timeline](/blog/pre-wedding-salon-timeline) covers hair, skin and nails together.

## Tell Your Therapist About Your Skin

Before any facial or treatment, mention sensitive skin, active breakouts, skin conditions, recent skin treatments or pregnancy. Your therapist will adjust the treatment or advise you on timing. If you have an ongoing skin concern, consult a dermatologist well before your event.
