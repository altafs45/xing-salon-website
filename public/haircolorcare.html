STEP ONE

The First 48 Hours Matter Most

Colour keeps settling into the hair shaft for a couple of days after your appointment — what you avoid in this window matters most.

Skip the Shampoo

Hold off washing for at least 48 hours. The cuticle needs time to close back around the colour, or pigment rinses out before it's bonded.

Go Easy on Heat

Freshly coloured hair is more porous. Skip flat irons and high-heat drying for a couple of days so the cuticle isn't forced open again.

Stay Out of the Pool

Chlorine and salt water strip new colour fast. If a swim's unavoidable, rinse with clean water and apply leave-in conditioner first.

STEP TWO

Build a Colour-Safe Routine

The right products and habits at home matter as much as the service itself.

Sulfate-Free, Cool Water

Sulfates strip colour fast — switch to sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner, and wash in cool water to keep the cuticle sealed.

Wash Less, Condition Weekly

Stretch washes to 2–3 times a week with dry shampoo in between, and add a weekly deep-conditioning mask to replace lost moisture and shine.

STEP THREE

Protect Colour From Everyday Fade

Sun and heat are the two biggest causes of colour fading between salon visits.

Shield It From the Sun

UV rays fade hair colour the same way they fade fabric. A leave-in UV spray or a hat on sunny days slows this down considerably.

Always Use Heat Protectant

A heat protectant spray before any dryer, straightener or curling iron keeps colour from dulling with each styling session.

QUESTIONS

Post-Colour Care FAQs

Wait at least 48 hours, and ideally up to 72 hours for deeper or fashion colours. This gives the cuticle time to close back down around the new pigment so it doesn't rinse straight out in the first wash.

Regular shampoos often contain sulfates, which strip colour faster than colour-safe, sulfate-free formulas. Switching products is one of the highest-impact changes you can make for colour longevity.

Washing less often, in cool water, with sulfate-free products — that combination alone makes the biggest difference to how long colour stays vibrant.
