Ask us on WhatsApp
Which hair color service is right for me?

Global color changes your overall shade. Highlights add dimension, balayage creates a blended look, and fashion colors are for bolder shades. Your colorist can recommend an approach after assessing your hair and the result you want.

How do I confirm the offer price?

Offer prices are mentioned in it's sections but if you need addtional clarifications, you can choose “Enquire about this offer” or message us on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm current pricing, what’s included, and availability before you book.

Does the price change with hair length?

The Global Hair Color offer is an any-length offer. Highlightd & Crazy Fashion Colors is priced per streak. Balayage starts from the advertised price and varies with length and density. You can confirm the current offer terms, price for you and any preparation needed with our team.

Can I color hair that has already been treated?

Tell your colorist about any previous color, bleach, henna, straightening or other treatments. We’ll assess your hair before recommending a process and may advise a strand test or preparation first.

Do I need to book an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Booking ahead helps us check availability with a colorist, particularly on weekends. Call or WhatsApp us, or leave an enquiry above.
